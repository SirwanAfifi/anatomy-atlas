Modifications for Anatomy Atlas:
- Selected muscle, tendon, and supporting bone geometry; removed internal systems.
- Normalized scale and orientation for a single interactive Three.js view.
- Added PBR muscle/tendon materials and authored 2K multiscale fibre normal and roughness maps.
- Applied bounded nonshrinking smoothing to muscle interiors, preserving material assignments and attachment tips.
- Softened regular muscle/tendon boundary curves by at most 0.75 mm, retaining curve endpoints and branch junctions.
- Subdivided broad low-density pectoral, deltoid, gluteal and trapezius surfaces once.
- Joined/smoothed original head, hand, and foot surface regions for context.
- Preserved individual muscle subdivisions and mapped 18 atlas muscle families.
- Corrected the source's swapped upper/lower trapezius labels using mesh placement.
- Compressed geometry with Meshopt using 16-bit positions, 14-bit normals and high-precision UVs; no geometry decimation.

The fibre normal map is illustrative material detail, not measured fascicle data.
The model is a teaching illustration, not patient-specific anatomy.
No organs, internal-ear assets, cranial nerves, or source Wikipedia definitions
are included in the exported model. Full upstream notices are preserved in
UPSTREAM-LICENSE.txt; exact provenance and statistics are in manifest.json.
